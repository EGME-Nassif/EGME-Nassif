### 🚀 What I'm Working On

Multi-robot testbed Design-is-ALL Lab

I founded the lab's hardware division about a year ago and still lead it. Our funded research project aims to build autonomous robots that can work independently and collaborate as a team, in the style of an assembly line.

Platform: TurtleBot3 Burger robots (Raspberry Pi 4) running ROS 2 Humble on Ubuntu 22.04
Networking: robots and a central control PC communicate over a Tailscale VPN using CycloneDDS
Where it stands: synchronized movement across robots, with a startup routine that brings two TurtleBots into sync in about 3 minutes
Next up: SLAM, line-following and keep-out zones using light and magnetic tape sensors, an overhead camera tracking system, and scaling to 4–6 robots in a dedicated arena
Future: NVIDIA Jetson boards, depth cameras, and more advanced LiDAR. Decentralized control is future work.
